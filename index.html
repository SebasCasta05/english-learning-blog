POST 01 / VIDEO ACTIVITY

My Virtual Job Interview

Introduction

Preparing for a job interview is an important step in professional development, especially when the interview is conducted in English. As a student interested in computer networks and technology, I understand that communication skills are just as important as technical knowledge.

My Experience

For this activity, I recorded a virtual job interview in English. During the interview, I answered questions about my academic background, personal interests, professional strengths, and future career goals.

I talked about my studies in Systems Engineering and Data Network Management, my interest in cybersecurity, and my motivation to continue developing my technical abilities. I also had the opportunity to describe some of my hobbies and the personal qualities that I consider important in a professional environment.

What I Learned

This experience helped me practice answering questions in English, organizing my ideas, and communicating with greater confidence. I also learned that preparing for an interview requires understanding my own abilities and explaining how they can contribute to a company.

Reflection

I believe that learning English can create new professional opportunities. Although speaking in another language can sometimes be challenging, activities like this encourage me to keep practicing and improving.

POST 02 / VIDEO ACTIVITY

Discovering a Tourist Place

Introduction

Traveling and discovering new places are interesting ways to learn about different cultures, traditions, and environments. English is especially useful when describing destinations and sharing travel experiences with people from different countries.

My Experience

In this activity, I prepared a video presentation about a tourist destination. My objective was to practice describing a place in English and organizing information in a way that other people could easily understand.

Through this exercise, I practiced presenting information, using descriptive vocabulary, and expressing ideas clearly. It was also an opportunity to connect language learning with the topic of tourism.

What I Learned

I learned that describing a tourist destination requires appropriate vocabulary, clear pronunciation, and a logical organization of ideas. These abilities are useful not only for traveling but also for everyday communication.

Watch the video on YouTube

Reflection

This activity reminded me that English is more than an academic subject. It is a practical tool that helps people share experiences, learn about different places, and communicate with others.

POST 03 / WRITING ACTIVITY

My Job Application Letter

Introduction

Writing a professional job application is an essential skill for anyone preparing to enter the labor market. A well-written letter allows applicants to introduce themselves, describe their qualifications, and express their interest in a particular position.

My Experience

In this activity, I wrote a formal job application letter for a Network Administrator position. I presented my academic background in Systems Engineering and Data Network Management and explained my interest in working in the technology sector.

I also described relevant abilities related to computer networks, technical support, programming, and problem-solving. My intention was to demonstrate how my education and personal commitment could contribute to a professional organization.

What I Learned

This activity helped me understand how to organize a formal letter in English. I practiced using professional vocabulary, presenting my qualifications, and expressing my motivation respectfully and clearly.

Reflection

I consider this activity especially useful because writing professional documents in English can help me prepare for future employment opportunities. It also motivates me to continue strengthening both my technical knowledge and communication skills.

POST 04 / WRITING ACTIVITY

Tim Berners-Lee and the World Wide Web

Introduction

Technology has transformed the way people communicate, study, and work. One of the most important developments in modern history is the World Wide Web, which made accessing and sharing information much easier.

My Experience

For this written activity, I prepared a chronicle about Tim Berners-Lee, the computer scientist who created the World Wide Web.

I researched important moments in his life and professional career, including his academic background, his work at CERN, and his proposal for a system that would allow researchers to share information through interconnected documents.

I also learned about the development of the first web browser, web server, and website, as well as the importance of keeping the Web accessible to everyone.

What I Learned

This activity helped me improve my English reading and writing skills while exploring a topic directly related to my academic interests. It also allowed me to understand how technological innovations can influence society and transform everyday life.

Read My Chronicle

Reflection

As a student interested in computer networks and technology, I find Tim Berners-Lee's contribution particularly inspiring. His work demonstrates how a technical idea can become a tool used by millions of people around the world.

Attitudes, Beliefs and Obligations

Introduction

Professional success does not depend only on technical skills. Personal attitudes, ethical values, responsibility, and teamwork are also essential for creating a positive academic and professional environment.

In this audio activity, I shared my opinions about attitudes, beliefs, and obligations in academic and work contexts.

I explained the importance of responsibility, discipline, respect, and collaboration. I also discussed how these values apply to the field of computer networks, where professionals must protect sensitive information, follow cybersecurity policies, and respond responsibly to technical problems.

What I Learned

This activity helped me practice expressing opinions, explaining responsibilities, and using English vocabulary related to professional behavior.

I also reflected on the importance of continuous learning, because technology changes constantly and network professionals must keep their knowledge updated.

POST 06 / AUDIO ACTIVITY

My Academic and Professional Plans

Introduction

Having clear academic and professional goals is important for personal growth. However, reaching those goals also requires flexibility, responsibility, leadership, and the ability to adapt to unexpected situations.

My Experience

For this audio activity, I explained my academic experience and future professional plans.

I discussed my interest in computer networks and cybersecurity, as well as my intention to continue developing my technical and communication skills.

I also reflected on the importance of making responsible decisions and responding effectively to challenges in professional environments.

For example, when unexpected network problems occur, professionals must analyze the situation, identify possible solutions, and work with their teams to restore services.

What I Learned

This activity allowed me to practice English conditional sentences and vocabulary related to leadership, decision-making, adaptability, and professional responsibility.

I also understood that solving technical problems requires more than knowing how technology works. Communication, patience, and teamwork are equally important.
